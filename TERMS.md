# Sharing terms

When you share a mapping to the ROTO-CONTROL Mapping Library, you agree to these terms.

1. **It's yours to share.** You made the mapping yourself, or you have the right to share it.
2. **It's public.** Your mapping goes into a public library that anyone can see and download.
3. **Others may use it freely.** Anyone may load, change and share your mapping, and doesn't need to credit you.
4. **What's published about you.** Only the credit name you choose (or "Anonymous") and the note you write. We don't collect your email address or anything else that identifies you.
5. **We may change or remove it.** We can correct its details (for example the plugin's developer or type), rename its file to match the library, tidy its layout, or decide not to publish it, or remove it later.
6. **Mappings only.** Share mapping files only. Never plugin files, presets, samples or anything else that belongs to someone else.
7. **As it is.** Community mappings aren't checked by us and come with no promise that they work. Use them at your own risk.

Questions or a mapping you'd like removed: open an issue in this repository.
